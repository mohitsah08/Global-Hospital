About Us

Our Story

Global Multi Speciality Hospital was founded with a simple yet profound mission: to bring world-class healthcare to the people of Gopalganj and beyond. What started as a small clinic has grown into a trusted multispeciality hospital, serving thousands of families with compassion and excellence.

Our team of experienced doctors, nurses, and support staff work tirelessly to ensure every patient receives personalised, dignified, and advanced medical care — because we believe every life matters.

  • Mission: Heal with Heart
  • Vision: Health for All
  • Core: Integrity & Excellence
  • Promise: Your Safety First

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is emergency service available 24×7?▼
Yes, our emergency department is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. We are always ready to provide immediate medical care.
What are the visiting hours?▼
Visiting hours are from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM daily. For ICU patients, visiting is restricted to immediate family members.
Where is the hospital located?▼
We are located at Ward No. 26, Friends Colony, Arar Road, Gopalganj, Bihar – 841428. Easily accessible by road.
How can I contact the hospital?▼
You can call us at +91 98352 92324, reach us on WhatsApp at the same number, or visit us in person during visiting hours.
